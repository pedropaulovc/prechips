## Actual alloy and wear-suitability HOLD

The video's 03:47–03:55 lead warning concerns introductory **12L14 steel**; it is not composition data or wear approval for the actual 360-brass project selected at 05:00. The [Copper Development Association C36000 table](https://alloys.copper.org/alloy/C36000), consulted for this bundle, lists **Pb 2.5–3.0%** for free-cutting brass. This is a published alloy range, not a test/certificate for the source scrap.
