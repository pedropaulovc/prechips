## Additional dimensions, selected as design nominals

- [Author's 2017 Magic Tube article](https://blondihacks.com/lathe-magic-tube/):
  12L14 material, 3.5-in **overall** size, 3-in stated interior depth and
  0.500-in cap head behind the shoulder. Its 0.500-in OD, 3/8-24 thread,
  0.375-in male stub, cap-first sequence and other shop details are **different**
  and are not asserted to describe the 2019 video.
- [Continental Steel 12L14 round-bar table](https://continentalsteel.com/viewitems/steel-bars/cold-finished-round-bar-type-12l14)
  and [item 12L14-6875](https://continentalsteel.com/item/steel-bars/cold-finished-round-bar-type-12l14/12l14-6875):
  11/16 in = 0.6875 in = 17.4625 mm. Adopted by the author as this design's
  final OD, not as evidence of the video's finished OD or catalog tube geometry.
- Explicit adaptation: author assigns 3.5-in **body**, 3-in cavity and 0.5-in
  head, making 4-in closed overall and 0.5-in base. This is not a claim that
  the article publishes a 3.5-in body. See README for every design allocation.

## Caption interpretation boundary

YouTube ASR is not a technical drawing. Obvious ASR corrections include
Quinn Dunki/Blondihacks (not “Quinton”/“blondie hacks”), 0.805/0.700-in
numbers (not time 8:05), thou/thousandths, die (not dye), TIR/runout,
seam (not seem), bevel/chamfer and deburr. The phrase rendered “15 30” near
08:54 is recorded as **15/32** in its narrated 1/2-20 tapping-drill context;
no tool body measurement is inferred. Source-mentioned engagement percentage
is not treated as a newly verified formula or thread-class acceptance.

Visible brands/classes are recorded only where established. Generic affiliate
shopping links do not supply missing tool sizes, machine model, material,
gauge calibration, fixture dimensions, chemical products or measured facts.
