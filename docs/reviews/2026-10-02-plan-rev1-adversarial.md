# prechips plan — adversarial review

## Verdict

The proposed gate cannot presently prove shop executability: its geometry checks are approximations, several required inputs are absent, and its pilots include a retired part (PLAN §§3–5, §7; finding 1). Deliver a narrowly scoped plan/inventory checker with explicit unknowns and a human-readable coordinate traveler before attempting generalized manufacturability certification. Do not approve milestones 2–4 as written; resolve the input contracts and stale consumer assumptions first.

Evidence notation: `PLAN` and `inventory` refer to the two prechips files; other local paths are relative to harmonic-analyzer. Expected signal/noise and proposed failure modes below are engineering judgments, not measured classifier performance. Both prechips files were read completely; no repository files were edited, no machining/build ran, and real STEP/FreeCAD reliability remains unverified because this checkout has no `cad/out/step/` directory.

## Fatal/major findings

1. **The pilot baseline is obsolete.** PLAN §7 calls `pivot-bushing` a ×19 production pilot and `rocker-arm` a flat profile. `cad/scripts/build_channel_assembly.py:32–34` explicitly retires the spacer bushings; `cad/config/dimensions.yaml:807–808` replaces them with integral rocker/lever hubs. `cad/config/parts/knife-mount.yaml:3–8` specifies hardened O1, 58–60 HRC, whereas `cad/docs/machining-dfm.md:51–55,103` discusses brass. Consequently, that DFM document is not a current process authority, the bushing cannot be a current consumer gate, and the rocker is not merely the old flat-plate operation. Select a current fourth pilot or explicitly freeze a historical bushing fixture; include knife-mount heat-treatment/finish sequencing.

2. **PMI and drawing cross-checks have no contracted input.** PLAN §§3.1,4 assume face-linked PMI; §5 accepts STEP/STL, plan and inventory only. `cad/scripts/export_models.py:118–150` explicitly exports **AP214 for colours**, not a semantic-PMI export contract; `_gtol_spec.py:1–10` describes native model/drawing annotations and pure Python specs, not STEP PMI delivery. There is therefore no demonstrated usable PMI input for the proposed finish/datum rules: mark them unavailable, not passed. A consumer-generated sidecar must bind feature IDs, nominal values, explicit/default tolerances, decimal precision, finish and datum relationships to the exact geometry/drawing revision. Drawing policy §§2–3,7 deliberately removes most formal datums and requires physical pickup origins; setup zeros cannot simply be inferred from datum letters. Comparing two values copied from the same spec is not an independent check of what the drawing actually prints.

3. **“Exact” accessibility is not an exact machinability test.** PLAN §3.1 samples faces and casts axial rays; a zero-radius ray can clear a passage the cutter cannot, miss an unsampled obstruction, or self-hit its source face. A vertical wall's axial ray is tangent, not a cutter-contact model; turning needs rotational/tool-orientation semantics rather than the milling rule. Reach from the setup's *highest* surface misclassifies unrelated bosses; overall length is not usable stick-out or cutting length. The final STEP also omits intermediate stock, so temporary obstructions and locating surfaces are unmodelled. Restrict initial checks to named operation classes, measured cutter/holder envelopes and explicit stock/setup states; return unknown for unsupported cases rather than universal clearance.

4. **The kernel choice is plausible; the promised robustness is unproved.** Candidate FreeCAD APIs are `Part.Shape.read`, `Faces`/`Surface`, `section` against a finite ray-edge, and `common`/`distToShape` against placed fixture solids ([TopoShape API](https://wiki.freecad.org/TopoShape_API)). OCC's [`IntCurvesFace_ShapeIntersector`](https://occt3d.com/dev/doc/refman/html/class_int_curves_face___shape_intersector.html) offers tolerance-controlled line intersections, but its existence does not establish a FreeCAD Python binding; prove the exact bundled adapter without adding another kernel. OCC explicitly documents tangency, tolerance, self-interference and parameterization failures ([Boolean limitations](https://occt3d.com/dev/doc/overview/html/specification__boolean_operations.html)). Require shape validation, error/unknown propagation and real exported-STEP cases. PLAN §4's claimed descriptor compatibility is also incomplete: `_gtol_spec.py:129–144,177–190` already requires spatial disambiguators for repeated cylinders/coplanar faces. Plane normal/offset or cylinder axis/radius alone cannot uniquely identify trimmed patches. Never select the first match; test split faces, seams, symmetric duplicates and re-exported geometry.

5. **Inventory uncertainty silently becomes a green exit.** PLAN §5 and inventory lines 2–4 downgrade all `verify:true`-dependent rules to warnings; exit zero requires only no blocks. Both machines, vise and dividing head are unverified; lathe collets have `present:null` (inventory lines 22,44,62,75,81–89). Thus “unknown machine envelope/holding capacity” can masquerade as executable. Separate `pass`, `fail`, `unknown`, `not_applicable`; required unknowns must prevent a readiness claim without pretending they prove physical failure. Define nested verification inheritance (`parallels…source.verify`) and distinguish absent from unmeasured tools. Inventory stores tool *sets*, not the individual flute lengths, holder assemblies and tap geometries the rules need. PLAN §5's `er32-12` and named tools do not resolve to this sample.

6. **The supplied example already fails elementary contract checks.** A read-only PyYAML parse of PLAN §5 produced four face strings—`cylinder(axis=A`, `r=5.0)`, `plane(normal=+Z`, `offset=4.5565)`—because commas in the flow sequence are unquoted. The same section's nearest-tool arithmetic is wrong: `0.2510 × 25.4 − 6.5 = −0.1246 mm`, not `+0.13 mm`. Keep the explicit no-substitution block policy, but separate comparison-unit normalization from tool substitution. Free-text `size_mm`, inspection strings, undefined axes A/B and no operation-to-feature association cannot support the promised automated rules. Use typed fields and explicit setup transforms.

7. **Physics has unsupported boundary conditions and unavailable inputs.** PLAN §3.3's cantilever expression assumes a prismatic beam/end load; `FL³/(48EI)` assumes a simply supported beam with a central load, not an arbitrary stepped shaft plus moving tool and compliant tailstock. The shaft is explicitly stepped (`cad/config/parts/cone-gear-shaft.yaml:10`). [Sandvik](https://www.sandvik.coromant.com/en-us/knowledge/materials/specific-cutting-force) defines specific force in the cutting direction and makes it dependent on chip thickness/rake; that is not automatically the radial load controlling diameter error. A radial displacement maps approximately to twice that displacement diametrally, not a direct tolerance comparison. Neither PLAN §5 nor the inventory supplies calibrated force components, actual hand feed, support stiffness or clamping force. Keep cited L/D/support advisories and optionally transparent sensitivity calculations; drop quantitative tolerance pass/fail and lot tool-change predictions. Cut length alone omits speed, heat, coolant, wear criterion and interrupted cutting; no recorded life exists here. Naming tape/wax/soft jaws (§3.2) does not validate grip strength, and reducing DOC without an explicit model does not justify a universal stick-out exemption.

8. **EL400 coupling is premature, and “positions exact” is false precision.** PLAN §§1,4,8 name a versioned preset format without identifying an existing importer. The live [EL400 README](https://github.com/pedropaulovc/el400) documents CNCjs position/probe mirroring, not a manufacturing-plan interchange. At commit `b374f49`, [`sdm.ts`](https://github.com/pedropaulovc/el400/blob/b374f49d6f01cfd5c0aac5ccde70ba8e98dd531d/src/stores/dro/features/sdm.ts) stores mm coordinates in session state, computes targets relative to work offsets and explicitly discards the SDM session on exit; [`droStore.ts`](https://github.com/pedropaulovc/el400/blob/b374f49d6f01cfd5c0aac5ccde70ba8e98dd531d/src/stores/droStore.ts) exposes dispatch/test state, not a stable file-import API. Define a neutral coordinate contract first: frame handedness, pickup procedure, units, absolute/incremental convention, radius/diameter mode, tool/edge-finder compensation, safe retracts and revision identity. Controller-reported coordinates do not prove actual motion accuracy, backlash compensation or touch-off accuracy. Keep cncjs motion macros out until modal-state and intervening-move safety are specified; they cross the plan/toolpath boundary.

9. **The determinism boundary is undefined.** PLAN §4's fixed samples/mesh tolerance do not define stable face ordering, tessellation ordering, import healing, near-boundary floating-point decisions, evidence ordering or JSON float serialization. Pin FreeCAD **and bundled OCC**, importer settings, platform/build, numerical tolerances, sampling algorithm/seed, schema/rule/table revisions and canonical serialization; bind STEP/STL identity, or derive the render mesh from the checked STEP. Do not use OCC object hashes as persistent IDs (TopoShape API documents reference-based `hashCode`). Separate canonical semantic-report hashes from rendered bytes. If image/PDF bytes enter the hash, also pin renderer/backend, fonts, locale, time zone, timestamps, PDF identifiers and compression; test fresh-process and supported-platform repetition. A doit dependency digest and a verdict hash solve different problems.

10. **Stock comparison is undefined process synthesis, not arithmetic.** PLAN §§2,3.5 asks for automatically enumerated stock forms, setups, waste and an “is this 4-axis?” answer. Geometry can compute a declared blank's volume; it cannot supply setup routes, casting allowances, brazing design acceptance, procurement prices, fixture labour or manual indexing effort. Indexed 3+1 work is not simultaneous four-axis machining. Accept explicitly authored candidate routes and show an unweighted Pareto table; do not declare a winner or minimum axis count. A brazed boss changes the design, not merely the stock, and needs more than a permissive drawing note (§9).

11. **Integration is not free, even when CAD leaves remain unchanged.** PLAN §7.5 proposes `cad/config/process/`; `_buildgraph.py:2047–2075` uses fine-grained `_config` access analysis with a recursive whole-config fallback. Unread process YAML avoids precise CAD-leaf keys, but any `**` fallback absorbs it (`dodo.py:1106–1118`); `check:math`, `check:config` and `check:recipe` also rerun through their broad YAML dependencies (`dodo.py:3161–3177,3232–3241`). Those check stamps are not CAD cache leaves: the narrow claim holds, not a blanket “nothing invalidates.” The new gate must explicitly depend on its plan, inventory, cited tables, checker/runtime, STEP and specification sidecar. Existing checks use flat `check:<name>` registration; the proposed `check:process:<stem>` needs deliberate generator/aggregate enrollment, not merely a directory (`dodo.py:3438–3475,3688–3689,3717–3721`). Fresh STEP currently comes from a COM export, so the checker is seatless, not its entire prerequisite chain. Feeding reports into the existing blind review also changes its image-only isolation/provenance contract (`machinist_review.py:25–34`); retain a blind drawing verdict and add a separately labelled process review rather than quietly contaminating it.

12. **The last physical defenses are mostly absent.** PLAN §§3.2–3.4 checks travel/swing, not the complete setup: quill stroke versus hole depth, drill insertion/removal room, spindle-to-table stack with vise/base/parallels/part/holder, actual soft limits, chuck-jaw swept diameter/stick-out, saddle/toolpost clearance, bar protrusion behind the headstock, tailstock collision and parting-blade radial reach. Inventory provides quill/headroom numbers but lacks the fixture/tool stacks (lines 15–16,38,65–81). Blind taps need full-thread depth, lead length, drill-point allowance and major-diameter breakout—not merely flute depth (`machining-dfm.md:72–92`; drawing policy §7). Require an operator setup/dry-clearance check and an inspection method with suitable verified gauges before cutting; `measuring` lists no pin gauges despite PLAN §5 requiring one. Bind first-article status to the exact part/plan/tool/material revision and human-authored logbook evidence; `book/AGENTS.md:10–16,27–30` forbids invented processes or promotion by plausible prose.

13. **Citation presence is not a testable rule specification.** PLAN §8 requires every numeric threshold to carry a citation, but §3.2 gives an uncited `3Ø` and undefined minimum grip, wall floor and footprint threshold; §3.3 gives `3–4` without selecting a limit or defining DOC reduction; §3.1 supplies no re-fixture budget. Each retained rule needs units, applicability, exact inequality/boundary behavior, source edition/page, uncertainty treatment and valid/invalid examples. A generic Handbook/Sandvik reference does not justify a material/tool/setup-specific threshold; otherwise implementations can disagree while all claiming compliance.

## Recommended cuts and reorderings

- **First:** reconcile pilot revisions and inventory, define typed features/frames/unknowns and the consumer sidecar. Resolve the retired bushing explicitly; do not claim four current pilots exist (§7; finding 1).
- **Smallest useful milestone:** merge restricted M1 with coordinate tables from M4 and minimal M5 wiring: four agreed pilot fixtures, inventory/sizing/order/blind-hole checks, cited inputs, canonical report and printable planned traveler. Demonstrate a missing sizing tool, wrong setup sign, insufficient tap-depth allowance and unsupported shaft support; include valid controls so an always-failing checker cannot satisfy §2's “known-bad per rule.” Measure warning burden on the actual good plans.
- **Next:** a FreeCAD spike on those exact STEP exports: unique descriptor resolution, bounding boxes and one operation-specific reach/collision check. Only then expand M2/M3 to necessary vise/chuck/support models. Defer universal coverage, ray thickness maps, force/tool-life gates and automatic stock-route synthesis.
- **Cut “while-you're-at-it” expansion:** every fixture family, EL400 import/motion macros, stock optimization and optional CAM simulation (§§2,6–7). Add adapters only after a neutral traveler is used successfully; packaging follows a release decision, not an unresolved §9 item.
